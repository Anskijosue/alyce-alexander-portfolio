Greetings! I am Alyce Alexander, a graduate of Florida Agricultural and Mechanical University's School of Journalism and Graphic Communications. I am fueled by an unwavering commitment to uncovering the stories that matter most.

With a keen eye for detail and a passion for storytelling, I am driven to shine a light on the untold narratives that shape our world. Armed with a pen, a camera, and a determination to seek truth, I am ready to embark on a journey of discovery, to challenge perceptions, and to amplify voices that deserve to be heard.
